## 3. Story write-up (paste into the portal)

Kiki is a baby Moon-Mouseling and an apprentice Starlight Weaver who lives in a teacup lined with velvet moss at the top of the Observatory Tree. I wanted a portfolio that feels like a tiny corner of the early web where somebody made something with their hands: pixel title bars, a marquee, a visitor odometer and a "best viewed at night" badge, all wrapped around a very soft, very shy character.

The page is built around one idea: Kiki reacts to you. Her name is a row of music-box keys you can play. She tilts toward your cursor, leaves a trail of stardust, and answers every poke. The scarf hides a secret (click it three times and she sneezes shooting stars), and if you boop her nose too fast she curls into a yarn ball, just like her character sheet says she does. Everything she makes is a toy rather than a screenshot: re-thread a blanket for a sleepy, dreamy, brave or giggly night, connect twelve stars into a constellation, brew a bedtime drink and sip it, then drag a yarn ball across the playpen and watch Kiki chase it.

Sound is part of the world too. The lullaby, crickets, birdsong and effects are all synthesized live in the browser, so there are no audio files, and the mute switch is always in the top bar. Day mode is "Pastel Morning Tea" and night mode is "Deep Starlight", and Kiki picks one from your clock. The page also pays attention to its own size: a live window readout in the marquee, a remark whenever you cross into a new size, and a giggling wobble if you drag the window edge around fast.

I am proudest of the scroll story, where a golden thread is stitched down the page with a little needle riding its tip, and of the sneeze Easter egg, where the sound is timed to the exact frame of the animation.
