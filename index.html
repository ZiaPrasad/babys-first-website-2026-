Kiki
What they are
A baby Moon-Mouseling with a round lavender body, big fuzzy ears, dewdrop eyes and a knitted scarf spun from moonlight. Shy, affectionate, soft-spoken and endlessly curious.
What they do
Apprentice Starlight Weaver. Kiki spins beam-threads from fallen stars into glowing night-blankets, so every creature in the forest feels safe on a cold night.

How Kiki became a weaver

Scroll down and follow the thread.

A cold star in the moss

One quiet night, Kiki found a fallen star lying cold in the velvet moss. Nobody else had noticed it. So Kiki wrapped it in yarn, the way you would a friend.

It started to glow

The star warmed up, then lit the whole clearing. The Elder Weaver saw the glow from high above and came down to see who had done it.

Up the Observatory Tree

“You have the hands for it,” said the Elder. Kiki packed one teacup, three yarn balls and the scarf, then climbed to the Loom Room to learn starlight weaving.

Every evening, a new blanket

Now Kiki weaves until the stars come out, then curls up in a velvet-moss teacup and falls asleep, ready to start again tomorrow.
